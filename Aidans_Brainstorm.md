# Project Concept: Technical Sales Competition & Recruiting Platform

## Overview
A virtual technical sales competition and recruiting platform—positioned as the **"GitHub + LeetCode" for Sales Engineering**—that bridges the gap between university engineering talent and B2B hiring managers. 

Students create free profiles, prepare for real-world industrial and enterprise cases using interactive architecture sandboxes and AI-assisted discovery drills, and compete in live, platform-hosted time slots judged directly by corporate engineers. Sponsoring companies pay a flat fee per competition or an annual subscription for automated tournament scheduling, direct judging access to pre-vetted engineering talent, and year-round searchable access to verified candidate scorecards, system diagrams, and recorded pitch clips.

---

## The Problem
* **For Hiring Companies:** Traditional resumes and coding tests fail to prove whether an engineer can run technical discovery, handle buyer objections, or translate complex architectures into business ROI. Campus recruiting is expensive, manual, and geographically limited.
* **For Engineering Students:** Technical sales (Sales Engineering / Solutions Architecture) is a lucrative career path, yet students have no centralized platform to practice realistic B2B technical cases or showcase verified customer-facing skills to employers.
* **Market Gap:** Current competitions rely on disconnected tools (Zoom, Google Forms, PDFs, Excel) that leave candidates with zero permanent portfolio. Existing sales-tech platforms target generic B2C/SaaS cold-calling rather than technical architecture and discovery.

---

## Core Platform Features (MVP Scope)

### 1. Pre-Competition Prep & Sandbox
* **Case Portal:** Digital distribution of technical RFPs, product datasheets, and buyer background packets leading up to competition dates.
* **Interactive Architecture Sandbox:** Drag-and-drop block diagramming tool allowing students to map out technical solutions (e.g., piping/sensor layouts, cloud/network topologies, bill-of-materials) and document engineering trade-offs.
* **AI-Assisted Discovery Drills:** Configurable buyer personas (text/voice or interactive roleplay modules) allowing candidates to practice technical discovery questions and objection handling against case constraints before live rounds.

### 2. Live Competition Engine
* **Automated Time-Slot & Bracket Routing:** Self-serve scheduling engine that matches corporate judge availability windows with student competitors, enforces strict round timers, and manages bracket advancement.
* **Split-Screen Judge Cockpit:** Integrated video room alongside a live, standardized grading rubric (e.g., *Technical Discovery Depth*, *System Architecture Accuracy*, *Objection Handling*, *Business ROI Framing*) with one-click timestamped feedback notes.

### 3. Year-Round Verified Talent Portfolio
* **Verified SE Profiles:** Post-competition profiles displaying percentile rubric scores, judge feedback highlights, submitted system diagrams, and short recorded technical pitch clips.
* **Recruiter Search & Filtering:** Sponsor dashboard allowing hiring managers to filter candidates by engineering major, graduation year, and verified rubric category scores.
* **Async Sponsored Mini-Cases:** Year-round custom technical prompts posted by corporate partners to pre-screen applicants prior to final-round interviews.

## Business Model & Go-To-Market

### Revenue Streams
1. **Per-Competition Sponsorship ($1,500 – $3,000 / event):** Includes judge seats, automated bracket scheduling, brand placement on the case, and candidate lead export.
2. **Annual Corporate Recruiting Tier ($5,000+ / year):** Multi-event judging access, year-round searchable talent database, and custom asynchronous pre-screening mini-cases.
3. **Student & University Club Access:** 100% free to maximize adoption across collegiate Sales Engineering clubs and engineering campuses.

### Initial Traction & Leverage
* **Built-In Distribution:** Leverage existing university Sales Engineering club networks and established relationships with companies actively recruiting collegiate SEs.
* **Validation Milestone ($5,000 Innovation Challenge):** Use initial seed funding to build the MVP scoring/portfolio portal, host an inaugural multi-university virtual SE competition, and onboard the first 3–5 paying corporate sponsors.
